Woods Group Dashboard: privacy

The Woods Group Dashboard is a private reporting tool prepared by The Co. Accountants & Business Advisors (Suite 1 / Level 10, 45 William St, Melbourne VIC 3000) for Woods & Co. Recruitment Pty Ltd and W&Co Search Pty Ltd. Access is limited to people approved by The Co.

What we receive when you sign in

When you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the dashboard. Your email address is checked against the list of approved viewers. Your name and picture are shown only to you, in the corner of the page.

How it is used and kept

  • Your email address and the time of each visit are recorded in an access log that only The Co can see, for security. Logs are kept by Google Apps Script under its standard retention.
  • We do not store your Google password, and we do not ask Google for access to your email, files, calendar or any other data.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for marketing.
